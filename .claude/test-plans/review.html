BEU-01 createOrder with a sub-scale amount yields an unfillable PENDING order Monetary BVA + regression pin

Given: CreateOrderRequest.amount carries @DecimalMin(0.0, exclusive), so 0.000000001 passes bean validation; OrderService.createOrder then normalizes to scale 8 and — unlike fillOrder — never re-checks positivity.

INT-05 Complete the concurrency pairwise matrix — amend must appear in a race Pairwise + State Transition

Given: OrderConcurrencyIT covers fill-versus-fill, fill-versus-cancel (partial) and fill-versus-cancel (full). Amend never appears in any race, even though it is one of the three actions ADR-0001 serializes and AC-13 names.

When: Using the same ExecutorService and CompletableFuture shape, two further races are run on a seeded order (amount = remainingAmount = 100, no fills): amend-versus-fill (amendOrder(id, null, 40) against fillOrder(id, 60)), and amend-versus-cancel (amendOrder(id, 1.20, 50) against cancelOrder(id)).

Then: amend-versus-fill: whichever order they land, the final document satisfies remainingAmount at or above zero and at most (amount minus the sum of fills), and a losing fill threw InvalidFillAmountException with Reason.EXCEEDS_REMAINING rather than being silently dropped. amend-versus-cancel: the final status is CANCELLED, and if the cancel won, the stored triggerPrice is still the original — an amend must never mutate an order after a cancel takes effect; the losing action threw OrderNotOpenException. Structure these as a parametrized pair, not a copy-paste of the three existing race tests.
